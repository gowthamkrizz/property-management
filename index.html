Frequently Asked Questions

Key details regarding acquisition protocols, ownership structures, and completion timelines.

What is the completion timeline and handover schedule for Stackly Kensington? +

Phase I of the Kensington Residences is fully completed with immediate occupancy available. Phase II Penthouse collections and the private Garden Villas are scheduled for phased handover in Q4 2026.

Are international buyers and sovereign wealth acquisitions supported? +

Yes. Our in-house legal and fiscal advisory partners coordinate sovereign wealth, corporate trust, and international individual transactions in full accordance with UK statutory compliance and discretion.

What private parking and EV charging provisions are included? +

Every residence includes dedicated subterranean secure valet parking with biometric access, high-speed 22kW smart EV charging bays, and automated license-plate recognition security.

Can residences be acquired with full bespoke turnkey European furnishings? +

Yes. Our Interior Atelier offers turnkey acquisition packages complete with curated European furnishings, custom art commissions, Poliform cabinetry, Gaggenau culinary appliances, and Bang & Olufsen integrated AV systems.

What 24/7 private concierge, security, and resident amenities are provided? +

Residents enjoy a 24/7 uniformed concierge desk, diplomatic-grade security protocols, private 20-meter heated swimming pool, vitality spa, Finnish sauna, sommelier wine vault, and private screening salon.
